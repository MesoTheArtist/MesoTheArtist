### OJA247 — Built for Sellers. Made for Buyers.

**[OJA247 on GitHub](https://github.com/MesoTheArtist/OJA247)**

OJA247 is a digital marketplace designed to help Nigerian vendors and small businesses establish their online storefronts, showcase products, and reach customers without the complexity of building an online store from scratch.

**Core features**

* Vendor onboarding and business verification
* Online storefronts and product management
* Shopping cart and checkout
* Payment integration with Paystack
* Customer accounts and order management
* Dispute management
* Administrative dashboard
* Referral and marketer systems

**Technology:** React, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, and integrated payment services.
